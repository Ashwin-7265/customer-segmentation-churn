The five insights that matter most for a decision

Ranked by how confident we can be in this sample — see the executive PDF for full detail
  • 1
    Contract type is the strongest churn signal High confidence 100% of month-to-month customers churned vs. 0% of annual/two-year customers (Fisher's exact p<0.001). Validate on the full base before acting.
  • 2
    Support tickets separate the groups almost perfectly Moderate–high Churned customers averaged 4.3 tickets (range 3–6); retained customers averaged 1.0 (range 0–2). Correlation, not yet proven causation.
  • 3
    Churn concentrates in the first 14 months Moderate Every churned customer left within 2–14 months; every retained customer had 15+ months of tenure. Partly definitional — early churners can't have high tenure.
  • 4
    Enterprise tier hasn't churned, but the group is small Moderate Basic churns at 71%, Pro at 50%, Enterprise at 0% — but tier overlaps heavily with contract type and tenure, so this may not be an independent driver.
  • 5
    Payment method shows a directional but weak split Low confidence UPI and debit-card users churned more than bank-transfer users, but cell sizes of 2–3 customers make this the least reliable finding — flag for monitoring only.
